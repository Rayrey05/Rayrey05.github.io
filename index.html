  • Setup. Turn on the camera, then Calibrate while looking at the screen normally. Postura offers to recalibrate at the start of a new session, since your seat or camera may have moved.
  • Tracking. Head down = studying. Head up or away = idle. Hit Start to make it count.
  • Earning. Studying banks break time. Looking up or away for 15s straight auto-starts a break, counting down from your bank — if it hits 00:00 it keeps going into debt (down to a limit of -15:00) until the break ends.
  • Manual mode. "Manual breaks only" turns off auto-breaks — you start breaks yourself with the button. A manual break can still run into debt the same way if it counts all the way down.
  • Glasses. Calibrate with your glasses on. Postura blends eyebrow position with eye position to soften the effect of lens glare, but strong reflections can still throw tracking off — even, front-facing light helps.
  • Keep it visible. If Postura is in a background tab or minimised and the camera stops updating, tracking pauses and study time isn't counted. Breaks keep counting.
  • Internet. Postura needs an internet connection to load its face detector each time you open it.
  • Privacy. Your camera video is processed on this device and is never uploaded or stored. Postura loads its face detector and fonts from public CDNs, which can see your IP address.
